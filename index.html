Last updated: [Dec 21, 2025]

This Privacy Policy explains how Golden (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects your information. Golden helps you arrive on time through calendar syncing, traffic-aware reminders, and optional location-based alerts. We collect only the minimum data required to deliver these features.

Golden does not require account creation unless explicitly stated in the App.

Automatically Collected Information

Golden may collect limited device and usage information such as device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and anonymous performance metrics. This data is used solely to maintain, improve, and secure the App.

Location Information (Optional)

If enabled, Golden may access approximate or real-time location solely to estimate travel time for active reminders such as “Leave Now” alerts. Golden does not track your location continuously and does not store historical location data beyond what is required to deliver the selected feature.

Golden does not sell or rent personal information. Limited data may be shared with service providers (such as mapping, analytics, or infrastructure services) solely to operate the App.

5. Data Retention

Golden does not permanently store calendar event content and processes event data transiently to deliver reminders. Data is retained only as long as necessary to operate the App.

6. Your Choices & Rights
  • Disconnect your calendar at any time
  • Disable notifications or location access in device settings
  • Cancel subscriptions through your app store
